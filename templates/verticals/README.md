# Vertical templates

Empty **structured** packs for studio “New from template”. They follow the four stages and eleven gates. They are **not** generate-ready and they do not fake hop-1.

| Id | Use |
|---|---|
| `education-lesson` | Clocked lesson beats (hook / teach / check) |
| `brand-promo` | Short product beat + talent lock |
| `short-drama-ep` | One episode of a short drama (verse/chorus-shaped map) |

Each folder has `vertical.json` (studio metadata), `pack.json` (builder round-trip), and markdown cards copied from `templates/`. Fill the blanks; export a zip from the pack builder when gates are green. Do not commit likeness stills or engine MP4s.
